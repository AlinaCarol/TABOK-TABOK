# Crossy Road Base (Godot 4 + C#)

1. Install Godot 4.4+ (the **.NET** build) and the .NET 8 SDK.
2. Open Godot -> Import -> select `project.godot`.
3. Press Build (hammer icon, top right), then Play (F5).

Controls: WASD / Arrow keys to hop. R / Space to restart after dying.

## Gameplay updates

- The camera follows the chicken more smoothly and tracks its horizontal movement; the Philippine Eagle is smaller.
- Bangka hull trim matches its hull color, and the outriggers sit at the water surface.
- Taho-man spawns are less frequent.
- Sidewalks across the maps now use grayer concrete colors and slab seams. Four-row roads can have marked pedestrian crossings.
- Added STOP signs and smaller, framed NO PARKING signs, sometimes beside an illegally parked tricycle.
- Waving pedestrians beside rivers face away from the water.
- Added random street events: "HOY! TABI!", brownouts, Chismis Alert, videoke, Barangay Tanod, Asong Kalye, and Tricycle Special.
- Chismis uses existing pedestrians who walk together to talk. During its event, the tanod chases only if you cross outside the marked crossing on a four-row road; stray dogs can also chase you, and tricycles may reverse.
- Added Filipino dialogue, including "Pre, ingat!", "Saan ka pupunta?", "Walang preno!", "Libre sakay!", "Naku po!", and "Tabi-tabi po!".
